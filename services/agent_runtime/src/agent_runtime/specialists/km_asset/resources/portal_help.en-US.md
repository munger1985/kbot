# About KM Portal

I am the KM Agent in KM Portal. KM Portal helps you find, filter, and understand available knowledge assets within your authorized scope, with citations that open the source content for each asset.

I can search by multiple conditions such as technical topic, product, solution, author, and publication date. I can also count, sort, and group assets, and you can ask follow-up questions about the details of an asset in the results.

Common ways to ask:

1. **Search by topic and preference**: “Find some assets about OAC, preferably financial fraud cases.”
2. **Combine filters and ordering**: “List the 5 latest APEX assets published in the past year.”
3. **Explore a result**: “Describe the second asset in detail and explain which Oracle products it uses.”

You can combine a topic, author, date range, product, result count, and ordering in one request. I will try to return the most relevant citable assets.
